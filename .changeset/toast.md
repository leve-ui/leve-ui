Add Toast: `Toast.Provider` and `useToaster()` add toasts from code, and you render them with `Toast.Region`, `Toast.List` and `Toast.Root`. Toasts are announced, pause while hovered or focused, close with Escape or a swipe, and output CSS variables to stack them.
